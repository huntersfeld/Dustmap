# Dustmap — project context

Hand-off notes for continuing this project in a new chat or project. Read this first; it explains what exists, how the code is built, the decisions already made, and what comes next.

**Live prototype:** https://claude.ai/artifact/DNWCJkheMFSz67dMPTWf5x (published from `app/dustmap.html`, shared as "Anyone with the link")

## What this is

Two related pieces of work:

1. **Pixel-art cowboy sprites.** Four characters translated from painted reference art (`reference/original_art/`) into tiny pixel sprites with 8-frame idle animations. They are the tokens used in Dustmap.
2. **Dustmap.** A browser-based battle-map editor for D&D-style tabletop games, set in a pixel-art Western desert. The DM draws shapes or brushes terrain and it fills in procedurally (ragged edges, shorelines, cliffs, scattered cacti and rocks, towns that fill with buildings). It is heading toward a multiplayer DM + players tool.

## The four cowboys (sprites)

All are tiny pixel art, hand-placed on a character grid for the smallest size, then animated by moving parts a whole pixel at a time.

| # | Name | Pose / details chosen | Idle animation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Harmonica | Huge hat and white mustache, gunslinger stance (arms out and down), harmonica in right hand | Breathing, hat lag, mustache tips flap, harmonica-hand flick |
| 2 | Wheat | Bearded drifter, duster, wheat stalk in mouth, relaxed arms, **hat pulled low** (eyes hidden) | Breathing, hat lag, wheat head dangles, duster tails flare |
| 3 | Cigarillo | Black frock coat, horseshoe mustache, gunslinger stance, **cigarillo held in hand**, pants lightened for contrast with coat | Breathing, hat lag, coat tails, ember flicker, looping smoke puffs |
| 4 | Wizard | Pointed floppy hat, long hair and beard, brown coat, satchel, **palm up at shoulder height with a blue flame** | Breathing, hat + floppy-tip lag, coat tails, procedural flickering blue flame |

**Game-ready format (`sprites/game_ready/`):** every sheet is a single row of 8 frames, each 32×32 px. Pivot is bottom-centre between the feet at (16, 31). 150 ms per frame. `all_cowboys_idle_atlas.json` describes the atlas. Smoke and flame pixels are semi-transparent (no outline) and only survive fully in the PNGs, not in transparent GIFs.

**Art conventions:** 1 px dark outline (`#281a18`-ish) around solid shapes; effects (smoke, magic, glow) have no outline; colours come from the painted references; animation offsets are whole pixels, with secondary parts lagging the body by 1–2 frames (hats only lag on the way *up*, so no gap opens between hat and head).

## Dustmap: features today

- **Map:** 16 px = one 5 ft square. Sizes from 20×14 up to 60×40 squares (⋯ menu, or zoom out and click the + tabs on each edge to add 10 squares on that side). Resizing keeps content in place and can be undone.
- **Camera:** trackpad two-finger scroll pans and pinch zooms; mouse wheel zooms in steps; right-drag, Space-drag, Pan tool (H) or dragging empty ground in Select pans; touch pinch/pan; `+` `−` zoom, `0` fits.
- **Layout:** the map fills the screen; everything floats over it. A tool rail on the left (icons only), a swatch tray at the bottom that shows only what the active tool needs, map name top left, undo / player preview / Play together / settings top right, zoom bottom right. Every button and swatch has a hover tooltip: name, shortcut and a one-line note. A status chip shows only while something is in progress (a shape being drawn, a sculpt, what's selected).
- **Tools (rail):** Select (V), Paint ground (B brush / D shape), Hills and cliffs (E: sculpt with brush or shape, Hill/Cliff style, drag up/down), Objects (O), Tokens (T: Party / Town / Enemies, the picked character opens into its colour variants), Fog (F), Measure (M), Ping (P). Pan (H) has no button: drag empty ground. Number keys 1–9 pick the numbered swatches in the tray.
- **Selection bar:** selecting a token, object(s) or shape shows Duplicate (Ctrl+D), Save as stamp (objects) and Delete just above it.
- **Right-click quick menu:** a right-click that doesn't drag (dragging still pans) offers Ping here, Measure from here, Duplicate/Delete what's under the cursor, Place the last token, Reveal/Hide around here. In the Objects tool right-click still turns the grid or stamp.
- **Terrain (Paint):** Sand, Scrub, Mesa, Water, Badlands, Road, Town, Stone, Mine rail.
  - Water gets foam, a wet-sand band and depth shading. Mesa gets rock faces on its south side, outlines and cast shadows. Stone is one material: flat it is cave floor; raised (Elevation or Sculpt) it becomes a cave wall with the dark wall texture on top and dark gray rock faces.
  - Roads get wheel ruts computed from the *merged* road shape (so crossings join cleanly). A road can only climb or drop one elevation level at a time; larger jumps are skipped and the road resumes beyond them; where it crosses a one-level cliff it renders as a ramp.
  - Town fills itself with small/medium/large buildings (shack about 2×2, saloon 4×4, hotel 5×4½ squares; a bit under true scale so towns read denser; a few big buildings go down first, then shacks fill the gaps) plus barrels, crates and troughs; buildings avoid roads, so a road through a town becomes a street.
  - Mine rail draws ties and two rails along the stroke path.
- **Hills and cliffs (E):** always sculpted. The tray has Brush / Shape, a style (Hill = soft slope, Cliff = rock face; key 1 / 2), and, when a hill or cliff is selected, a ↓ level ↑ stepper. Levels run −3…+3 (`SLAD`); anything below ground is a pit with rock walls whatever the style. Raised cliffs get rock faces coloured by the ground underneath (red for mesa, purple for badlands, dark gray for stone, tan otherwise); hills get a graded south slope, lit north/west edges, a shaded east edge and a soft cast shadow; lowered land renders as a recessed pit (inner wall, shadow, darker floor); lowered water just gets darker.
- **Sculpt brush (tool `sculpt`):** in one motion, drag over an area, hold still about 0.38 s (a ring fills) or press Shift, then drag up/down to set its level, starting from the most common height under it. A raised stroke is shifted up by its face height (`sculptLift`/`faceLen`) so the foot sits on the painted stroke. The new stroke stays selected so ↑ ↓ adjust it.
- **Sculpt shape (tool `draw` with a height paint):** click corners, Enter; the shape lands one level above what's under it and stays selected. Press-and-drag on any hill or cliff (shape or stroke) moves it up/down a level per ~30 px (`setOpLevel`; the outline itself doesn't move); a click without dragging starts a new shape there. In Select, a selected hill or cliff gets the style swatches, the stepper, and ↑ ↓.
- **Brush sizes:** Small 3, Medium 6, Large 12, Extra large 22 (`BSIZES`); [ and ] fine-tune.
- **Brush:** small gaps fully enclosed inside a single stroke fill automatically (up to about 8·r² px).
- **Objects:** the tray has Decor / Lights / Interact tabs (like the Tokens tray's Party / Town / Enemies); number keys count within the open tab and a Ctrl-click mix can span tabs. buildings, well, wagon, trough, barrels, crates, boulder, cactus, rock, dead tree, ore cart, lantern (glow), timber supports, rubble, crystals (glow); clutter (scattered papers, scroll, books, potions, sacks, table, bedroll, hay bale), dungeon & graveyard (skulls, cobweb, mushrooms, stalagmites, banner, coffin, gravestone) and a key. Objects listed in `LOOKS` (scroll, books, cobweb corner, banner colour, coffin, gravestone, key, chest) change look from the right-click menu, and new ones of that kind take the look picked last. Grid picker (Google-Docs-style 6×6) places arrays with adjustable spacing; arrays move as a group (Alt grabs one).
- **Random spots / fire:** the place-many popover has Grid | Random spots; Random spots drops N objects (1–30) at random inside an area N squares wide, a new layout every click (R re-rolls). Torch and Campfire objects draw an animated flame and a warm glow each frame (`drawFires`, between the objects layer and the fog, so fog still hides them).
- **Dynamite:** Objects → *Mine & cave* → Dynamite. The DM lights a bundle with a double-click in Select, or by selecting it and pressing Enter / *Light fuse* (a multi-selection lights them all). The selection bar also sets the blast width (`br`, radius 1–4 squares, default 2, so 4 squares across; shown as a dashed ring while selected) and depth (`bd`, 1–3 levels, default 2). After a 1.5 s fuse it explodes: the bundle goes, a crater is added as an ordinary height op (`{kind:'poly',layer:'height',h,s,blast:1}`, a ragged circle at the most common level under the blast minus `bd`, never below −3; on a two-level plateau the default blast reaches the ground), 3–5 rocks (2 rubble on stone) land around the rim, and other dynamite inside the blast goes off a moment later. One undo takes back each blast. Visuals are screen only (`drawBlasts`: flash, fireball, shockwave ring, flying dirt with fake height and gravity, sparks, smoke, scorch, a small screen shake; reduced motion / animation off gets a plain flash) and are hidden by fog. In a session only the DM can light fuses; the DM's presence carries the last 8 fuse/blast events (`bm:[{n,f,x,y,r,s}]`, f 1 = fuse, 0 = boom) and players' pages play the ones from the DM, while the crater arrives with the map. Code: the `// ===== dynamite =====` section of `dustmap_src.html`; test `app/tests/dynamite_test.js`.
- **Kill:** in Select, the DM picks a townsfolk or enemy token and presses **K** or *Kill* in the selection bar (Party characters and tokens a player plays can't be killed this way). The token comes off the map at once and a screen-only death plays where it stood (`killOver`/`killUnder`) in the style the bar's **Death** button picks (remembered per browser, `localStorage['dustmap-killstyle']`): *Fall* is a hit flash, a red tint, a spray of blood drops thrown with fake height and gravity (the dynamite dirt model), a puff of red mist, the body toppling over at its feet, a moment lying there, then a fade; *Explode* is a white flash, a red burst and ring, the body bursting into 4×4 chunks of its own sprite that fly, land, bounce and lie there, a much bigger spray, a cloud of red mist and a small screen shake. The drop button toggles *Leave blood* (default on, `localStorage['dustmap-killblood']`): a pool spreads and, once the body is down, stays as an ordinary object, `blood` for a fall and the bigger `gore` splat for an explosion (both in the Objects tray under Props). One undo brings the token back without the blood. Animation off / reduced motion gets a red fade and the pool straight away. In a session the DM's presence `bm` log carries `{f:3,x,y,s,d,b,e}` (sheet, seed, blood, explode) and players' pages play the same death; the removal and pool reach them with the map. Fog hides it.
- **Next hit kills / throwing:** the selection bar's crosshair button marks a townsfolk or enemy token killable (`kl:'f'` fall or `'x'` explode, following the Death button; saved with the map, never in `live/tokens`, so players don't see it). The DM sees a pulsing red crosshair over its head. In a live session a player in Select clicks a townsfolk or enemy token and their own token throws something at it (rock, bottle or knife, random), auto-aimed in an arc with a ground shadow; the hit flashes the target white then red with a few blood drops. Throws go on the player's presence (`th:[{n,id,fx,fy,tx,ty,sd}]`, last 6) and every page plays them; the DM's page judges the hit, and a killable target dies with its marked death (which then reaches everyone like a DM kill). Players also get an **Attack** tool (X, player only, next to Move / Measure / Ping): drag a line and release, and their token flings something that way, as far as the drag (2 to 12 squares, `AIM_MIN`/`AIM_MAX`), stopping at the first townsfolk or enemy token on the path (`aimPath`); a miss lands at the end in a puff of dust (`id:''` in `th`). While dragging, the player sees a dashed aim line and a red ring on whoever is in the way. A click on an enemy throws straight at it, as in Move. Code: the `// ===== kill =====` section of `dustmap_src.html`; tests `app/tests/kill_test.js` and `app/tests/kill_mp_test.js`.
- **Select:** click to select; a terrain shape only moves when dragged *after* it is selected (otherwise the drag pans). Drag corners to reshape. Auto-placed buildings, props, cacti and rocks can be picked up individually (they become normal objects and the generator leaves the spot empty). Shift-click / Shift-drag box for multi-select; **Save as stamp** stores the selection as a reusable stamp (saved per browser).
- **Tokens:** the four cowboys plus 13 townsfolk and 11 enemies, each with colour variants (79 sheets), all animated. Place them from the **Tokens** dropdown at the top of the Objects tool (the grid picker drops a group; a slider in the grid popover sets the gap, 0–6 empty squares between tokens, default 1, the same layout every click); in Select, click a token to select it and press Delete to remove it. They snap so their standing point (shadow) lands exactly on a square centre, edge midpoint or corner; a diamond shows the target while dragging; Alt places freely.
- **Token tray:** one swatch per character; a character with colours opens them in a column above its swatch (↑ ↓ step through them, Esc closes). Ctrl/Cmd-click tokens to mix them: a new mix starts with "Scatter randomly" on, so a click on the map scatters one of each (or as many as the grid picker has squares) on random spots of the gap-spaced lattice around the cursor (jittered only at gap 0), re-rolled after every placement. With Scatter randomly off, a mix fills the picked squares in order (one row, one of each, on a single square), evenly spaced. "Scatter randomly" works for a single token too. `placementTokens()`, `tokGap`. Ctrl-click in the Objects tray starts an object random mix the same way.
- **Keyboard steps:** the arrow keys or WASD move a token one square per press (the DM: the token selected in Select; a player: their own token, nothing to select). Two keys together step diagonally (one square, 5 ft), and so does left then up pressed one after the other: drawn through the square and counted once. The count is a loose distance from where the walk began: a step onto, or next to, an earlier point of the path cuts everything after that point, so backing up erases the line (forward then back counts 0). Holding a key keeps walking. Enter clears the path and count. The steps since the walk began show beside the token ("6 squares · 30 ft") with a dotted path; in a session the count and path go on presence (`ks`, only for tokens players can see) so everyone sees them, fading 20 s after the last step. The count starts over on another token, a drag, someone else moving the token, or Esc. One undo takes back the whole walk. Steps reveal fog like walking, set off trigger zones (`zoneStep`) and trigger doors (`portalStep`) like a dropped token. A player's steps go out as ordinary `mv` moves; the DM's page doesn't treat a player's own committed move as a change they missed (`mp.plMv`), so quick steps aren't dropped as stale.
- **Player aura:** in a live session a player's own token has a soft gold glow, a ring at its feet and a small marker overhead (screen only, not in snapshots); tokens other players picked show a faint ring.
- **Signs:** Objects → *Signs* → Signpost. Placing one opens a small editor under it: the message (up to 300 characters), the script it is written in (Common plus 15 D&D scripts: Abyssal, Celestial, Deep Speech, Draconic, Dwarvish, Elvish, Giant, Gnomish, Goblin, Halfling, Infernal, Orc, Primordial, Sylvan, Undercommon), *Players can read the translation*, and the board (plank, arrow right, arrow left). Double-click a sign in Select (or select it and press Enter / *Edit sign*) to edit it again; one undo takes back a whole edit. Hovering a sign shows it as a wooden card; on touch, a tap pins the card. Common is plain Pixelify Sans text. The other scripts are pixel glyphs traced from the alphabet charts in Hunter's "D&D Language and Script" Google Drive folder (one chart per language, each naming the dafont font it shows) by `sprites/generator_scripts/sign_scripts/extract_glyphs.py`; the result is inlined in the page as `SIGN_GLYPHS`. Charts that share a glyph between letters (Celestial and Giant: C/K, G/J, W/Y, U/V; Orc; Primordial) map every letter to it, and Primordial's digraphs th, ts and ph get their own glyphs. Digits and . , ! ? - ' : use a small 3 × 5 set in the same ink. The charts are not in the repo (about 2.3 MB each); re-run the extractor on a download of the folder to change the tracing. The DM always sees the translation under the script; players see it only when the sign allows it. Players can't read a sign standing in fog, and never see blank signs.
- **Doors, stairs and entrances:** Objects → *Doors & stairs*: Door, Stairs down, Stairs up, Cave entrance (one square each, snapped to the square they're placed on) and **Landing spot** (DM only: drawn as a dashed ring with an arrow on the DM's screen, never into the map image, so players and snapshots never show it). Placing a door opens its editor (double-click it in Select, Enter, or *Edit door* in the selection bar to reopen): its look, **Leads to** (another map in the library, or *Another spot on this map*), **Arrive** (automatic, a Landing spot on that map, or beside one of its doors), *Opens when a party token steps on it* (default on), *Locked for players*, and *Go through now*. One undo takes back a whole edit.
- **Trigger zones:** Objects → *Doors & stairs* → *Trigger zone*: an invisible box of whole squares (default 2×2), drawn only on the DM's screen as a dashed purple box with its name (never in the map image, snapshots or players' pages). Placing one opens its editor (double-click, Enter or *Edit trigger* to reopen): name, size (− / + wide and tall), **Reveal**: *An area I mark* (the default for new zones: **Mark area…**, then drag boxes of squares on the map; a click on a box removes it; only those squares are cleared, shown to the DM as amber boxes while the zone is selected), *The whole hidden patch it touches* (the fog inside or right beside the zone and everything hidden connected to it; zones made before marking existed keep this), or *Nothing*, **Link…** (then click dynamite, doors, torches or campfires on the map; lines show the links), what to do with linked doors (lock / unlock / send the party through the first one), a message everyone sees, *Goes off* once (then it's spent, with Re-arm) or every time, and *Ask me first* (a Fire / Skip card for the DM). A party token that enters the zone from outside sets it off: the DM's drags follow the whole drag path; players' drags follow their ghost on the DM's page. *Set it off now* tests it. Torches and campfires can be put out from the selection bar (`off:1`) so a zone can light them. Zones sit behind everything else for clicks, and only move once selected (dragging over one still pans). In a session only the DM's page fires zones; fog, fires and doors reach players with the map, and the message goes in the DM's presence `bm` log as `{f:2,m}`.
  - **Going through** moves the party (every Party character, plus any token someone plays in a session) off this map and opens the destination with them standing on free squares around the arrival spot, closest first (the token that walked in, then played tokens). Automatic arrival: a door there that leads back here, else that map's first Landing spot, else its middle. Beside a door means one square toward the middle of the map, never on the door. Copies of the same characters already on the destination are replaced. Walk reveal clears fog around the arrivals if it's on.
  - Triggers: a party token dropped on a door (DM drag, or a player's committed move); *Go through* in the selection bar or editor (works on locked doors); a player clicking a door while their own token is within one square (sent on presence as `go:{id,n}`, checked by the DM's page). A message says where the party went, with **Take it back** for a few seconds (returns everyone to where they stood, the walker to where its drag started). Hover shows the DM where a door leads; players see how to use it.
  - In a session it's the DM's page that switches maps, so the normal push sends the new map and tokens; players fade and re-fit when the map's name or size changes.
  - Saved fields on the object: `id`, `to` (library map id or `'here'`), `toN` (that map's name, the fallback when ids change after export/import), `at` (id of a Landing spot or door on the destination), `walk:0` (doesn't open on stepping), `lock:1`. Landing spots carry an `id`. Tests: `app/tests/portals_test.js` (solo) and `app/tests/portals_mp_test.js` (live session, mocked).
- **Tour map:** ⋯ → *Open the tour map*, or *Tour map* in the Maps dialog, adds "Dustmap tour" to the library: a 40×28 map with a zone per tool (paint, hills, town, mine, fog-hidden cave, badlands for measuring) and a sign in each explaining that tool (also new maps, torches and campfires with Random spots, fog double click and walk reveal, the token tray), a campfire and two torches, plus signs in Elvish (translation allowed), Dwarvish and Infernal to show scripts, and stairs down into the fogged cave (to a Landing spot inside) with stairs back up. Built by `tourMap()`; update its sign text when tools change. **It's also the default:** a first visit (nothing saved in that browser) opens the tour as "Dustmap tour" instead of the starter example; returning visitors keep the map they last had open. Tests written against the starter example set `window.__starterExample=true` in an init script.
- **Cave dungeon (Rattlesnake Deep):** ⋯ → *Open the cave dungeon*, or *Cave dungeon* in the Maps dialog. A ready-to-play 60×40 stone map built by `caveMap()`: entry adit (stairs up to the tour map), bandit camp with a captive miner, a hidden stash up a crawl, a tripwire (dynamite, asks first), mine works with rails, skeletons and a sandworm, a crystal grotto with a chasm, a lake cavern with an island chest and a cave mouth out to the tour map, a hall of pressure plates that locks behind the party, and the vault. A chute (stairs down) in the mine works drops into the grotto. Everything but the adit starts under fog; the zone at each room's way in reveals the squares marked for that room (`rv:2`, `ra`), so one room opens at a time. Walk reveal is on (radius 3). The DM key is three signs in the fogged pocket at the top-left corner. The tour map has a cave entrance in its mine leading here (`tour-deep`) and a landing (`tour-deep-out`) where the cave mouth comes out.
  - **Built-in maps:** `BUILTIN` maps names to builders (`Dustmap tour`, `Rattlesnake Deep`). A door whose destination isn't in the library but whose `toN` names a built-in map adds that map when the party goes through (`builtinAdd`), then points the door at it.
  - **Plate puzzle:** flame → wave → star. Zones can link other zones: when a zone goes off, every linked zone that is off or spent is switched on and re-armed. A zone with `off:1` (editor: *Off until a linked trigger switches it on*) never goes off by stepping until then; the DM sees it labelled "(off)". The wave and star plates start off; the star plate unlocks the vault door and the hall door and lights the vault torches. Skull and blank plates are dart traps (messages, every time).
  - **Treasure & traps objects:** Treasure chest (v % 3: closed, open with gold, iron-bound), Gold pile, Pressure plate (v % 5: blank, flame, wave, star, skull), Bones (`lootSprite`).
  - Zone reveal follows squares walk reveal already cleared (`fogWalked`), so a room still opens when the token that sets a zone off has cleared the fog around the zone itself. Test: `app/tests/cave_dungeon_test.js`.
- **Measure:** square-to-square distance in feet and squares, 5e default (diagonals count 5 ft).
- **Pointer:** click for a radar ping; press and drag for a pointer with a fading tail.
- **Map library:** the **Map: <name>** button in the header opens the list of maps saved in this browser. New map opens the **New map** dialog (also the **+** next to the map name, or **N**): name, ground (Sand, Scrub, Mesa, Water, Badlands or Stone filling the whole map) and size; the last ground and size are remembered (`localStorage['dustmap-newmap']`), and the four cowboys start near the middle. The ⋯ menu's **Ground** changes the open map's base later (undoable). Each map row has Open, Rename, Duplicate, Export, Delete (two-click confirm; the only map can't be deleted), Import file (one or many, or drop files on the dialog), Export all maps. Import never overwrites: every imported map is added as a new entry (names get " (2)" if taken), and a single imported map opens. If the page can't save files, the export text appears in a "Move maps as text" box to copy, and pasted text imports from the same box. Switching maps clears undo history.
- **Fog of war (F):** the DM hides and reveals squares with a brush (1–11 squares across) or a box. A click or drag paints what the tray's Hide/Reveal toggle says (Hide by default); a double click, or double click and drag, paints the opposite (and one undo takes the whole double click back); Shift also paints the opposite. Hide everything / Reveal everything. **Players reveal as they walk** (tray toggle, `st.fogWalk`, saved with the map): the DM's page clears fog around each player-held token along its path, out to `st.fogWalkR` squares in every direction (DM sets it with − / + next to the toggle, 1–10, default 4), live while the player drags (outside a session, the four party cowboys count). The DM sees hidden squares under a hatched veil and can preview the player view; players see a dark drifting mist with soft, irregular edges (`mistUpdate`: 4 texels per square, cached blurred edge falloff plus two scrolling noise layers, redone about 10×/s, frozen when token animation is off or reduced motion is on; hidden squares never drop below 94% opacity), and tokens standing in hidden squares are never sent to them (tokens handed to a player always show). Fog saves with the map, undoes, and moves with map resizes (new squares start hidden). In a session it is published on its own document (`live/fog`), so a reveal never resends the map. Limitation: the terrain and objects under fog are still in the shared map data, so a player who digs into the database could see the layout; tokens are not.
- **Layout:** the page is one screen with no page scroll. Header: map name (opens the library), Undo, Play together, and a ⋯ menu holding map size, edge roughness, Reroll details, Load example, grid and animation toggles, Snapshot and Clear map. Left panel: an icon grid of the nine tools (Fog sits next to Objects and is DM-only), then only the options for the active tool (Objects has Objects / Tokens tabs). A one-line status chip sits over the map's bottom-left corner. Helper text was cut deliberately; longer explanations live in button tooltips.
- **Other:** Undo (Ctrl/Cmd+Z), Reroll details, Clear map (two-click confirm), Load example, Snapshot (renders an image the user right-click saves), 5 ft grid toggle, edge roughness slider, token animation toggle. Maps autosave to `localStorage`.

## How the code works (`app/dustmap_src.html`)

One self-contained HTML file: inline CSS and a single IIFE script, no libraries. Fonts: Pixelify Sans (brand) and Work Sans (UI) from Google Fonts. Single dark theme by choice.

### State (saved to `localStorage['dustmap-v2']`)

```
st = {
  v: 3, cols, rows,            // map size in squares (W = cols*16, H = rows*16 px)
  seed, rag,                   // detail seed; edge roughness 0–9
  base,                        // terrain index under the whole map (BASES: 0,1,2,3,4,7); missing = sand
  ops: [ { kind:'poly'|'stroke', layer:'ground'|'height',
           t (terrain index) | h (level), s (0 hill / 1 cliff),
           pts:[[x,y]...], r (stroke radius), sid (per-op random id) } ],
  objects: [ { k, x, y, v, g? } ],   // centre position; v = variant; g = group id
                                     // doors/stairs/entrances also carry id, to, toN, at?, walk?, lock?; landing spots an id
                                     // zones: id, w, h, nm, rv (2 marked areas ra:[[c0,r0,c1,r1]] squares inclusive / 1 touching patch / 0), tg[], dr?, msg?, rep?, ask?, done?, off?; torches/campfires off?
                                     // signs (k 'sign') also carry txt, lang (script id) and tr (1 = players see the translation); v 0–2 = board
                                     // dynamite (k 'dynamite') may carry br (blast radius, squares 1–4) and bd (levels down, 1–3)
  tokens:  [ { s (sheet 0–3), x, y (standing point), o (frame offset) } ],
  skip: [ "prefix:x:y" ],            // auto-placed items the user picked up
  fog: null | "0101..."              // one char per square, row by row; '1' = hidden from players
  fogWalk?: true                     // players reveal fog as they walk
  fogWalkR?: 1..10                   // how far, in squares each way (default 4)
}
```
Older saves are migrated (`migrate()`); stamps live separately in `localStorage['dustmap-stamps']`.

### Map library and file format

`st` is always the open map and still saves to `dustmap-v2`. Every map, the open one included, is also stored as `localStorage['dustmap-map:<id>']`, and `localStorage['dustmap-library']` lists them: `{ cur: id, maps: [{ id, name, cols, rows, updated }] }`. `save()` writes both. On first load the existing `dustmap-v2` map becomes the first entry. Map names live only in the library, not in `st`.

Export files are JSON named `<name>.dustmap.json`:

```
{ "format": "dustmap", "kind": "map", "version": 1, "name": "…", "exported": "ISO time", "map": { …st… } }
{ "format": "dustmap", "kind": "library", "version": 1, "exported": "…", "maps": [ { "name": "…", "map": { …st… } } ] }
```

A bare `st` object also imports. Every map read from storage or a file goes through `libClean()`, which runs `migrate()`, clamps size/seed/roughness, and drops ops, objects or tokens with a bad shape (unknown extra fields are kept). For Phase 4, the `map` object is the unit to share: a DM can load an exported file into the library and push that `st` to the shared `db` document.

Exports go through the artifact `downloads` capability when the page has it (`claude.use('downloads')`), a normal browser download when opened as a local file, and the copyable text box otherwise. **Publish the artifact with `capabilities: {downloads: true}`** so Export saves a file there. Stamps are not part of map files (they stay per browser).

Terrain indices: 0 sand, 1 scrub, 2 mesa, 3 water, 4 badlands, 5 road, 6 town, 7 stone, 8 retired (old Cave wall; `migrate()` turns each such op into Stone plus a two-level cliff over the same shape), 9 mine rail.

### Rendering pipeline

1. **`rasterize(ops)`** turns the op list into per-pixel arrays: `L` (terrain), `Hm` (height), `Sm` (edge style), `own` (which op owns the pixel). Height ops are applied first, then ground ops (so roads can check heights). Edges are roughened by displacing each sample with value noise (`vn`) scaled by the roughness. Rails are not rasterized; they are drawn as geometry later.
2. **`paintMap(img, R, seed)`** colours every pixel: base textures, scrub/town/road/stone edge dithering, shorelines and depth, mesa and cave-wall faces/outlines/shadows, the elevation pass (faces, rims, pit walls, cast shadows, ramps), water ripples, rails, then auto-placed content: town buildings and props (per town op), and scatter. It returns the list of auto-placed items so Select can pick them up.
3. **Sprites:** every object is drawn once into a cached sprite (`sprite(k, v)`) with an automatic outline and a soft translucent shadow, then blended onto the map with `drawObject()`.
4. **Layers on screen:** terrain canvas (`tcan`) → objects canvas (`ocan`) → animated tokens → grid → overlays (selection, drafts, measure, pointer) drawn in screen space. Camera: `Z` = screen px per map px, `cam` = map px at the view's top-left.

### Partial redraw (important invariant)

Edits call `markOp(op)` / `markRect()` instead of redrawing everything. `renderRegion()` rasterizes and paints only the changed rectangle plus a 24 px margin, using global offsets `OX/OY` so **every noise lookup, pattern and scatter decision uses absolute map coordinates**. Scatter and ripples are placed one candidate per small cell, decided by a hash of that cell's absolute position, so an edit never reshuffles details elsewhere. A town touched by an edit is re-rendered whole (its building layout depends on the whole town shape). The test `verify_partial_redraw.js` checks a partial redraw is pixel-identical to a full redraw; **keep it passing** when changing paint code (any new `%`, parity or noise term must use `x+OX`, `y+OY`).

Full redraws still happen for: load, Reroll, roughness, resize, and undo that changes seed/roughness/skip. Typical edit: 40–130 ms. Full redraw: 0.3–0.5 s (40×28) and slower at 60×40.

### Build and test

- Edit `app/dustmap_src.html`, then `python3 app/build_dustmap.py` to produce `app/dustmap.html`.
- Tests use Node + Playwright with Chromium (`npm test`, or `node app/tests/verify_partial_redraw.js`). They load `app/dustmap.html` relative to the repo, write screenshots to `app/tests/output/`, and exit non-zero on failure.

### Platform notes (claude.ai artifacts)

- Scripts only from cdnjs / jsDelivr / unpkg; no network fetches; `alert/confirm/prompt` don't work (use in-page confirms); downloads need the `downloads` capability; `localStorage` is per viewer and per browser.
- Multiplayer building blocks available to this account: **`room`** (live messages and presence for whoever has the page open: pointers, pings, rulers) and **`db`** (shared, persistent documents with access rules: the shared map, roles). Both need the page declared with those capabilities; `db` needs signed-in viewers with access to the artifact.

## Decisions and preferences so far

- The user prefers the **tiniest sprite size** and chooses from small sets of visual options before animating.
- True-scale battle maps (one person = one square) over a compact look; buildings were scaled up rather than tokens shrunk, then trimmed back about 20% so towns feel denser (exact scale given up for buildings only).
- Elevation should feel physical: pits recess into the ground; roads obey one-level steps.
- Interaction choices: a pause (dwell), not angle or speed, switches Sculpt from selecting to raising; terrain shapes must be selected before they can be dragged.
- Physics engine (pour rocks, explosions, craters) is **parked** as a nice-to-have.

## Multiplayer (Phase 4, "Play together")

One session/roles layer (`mp*` functions in `dustmap_src.html`) runs on either of two transports with the same db / room / user shapes:

1. **Firebase (the main way to play, chosen by Hunter).** The page is served from Hunter's Vercel site at `/dustmap` (Portfolio repo, `app/dustmap/route.ts`), which fetches `app/dustmap.html` from this repo's `main` and caches it for 5 minutes, so merging here is all it takes to update it. claude.ai artifacts can't reach outside servers, which is why it isn't the artifact. `app/firebase_config.json` (the public web config) is inlined by `build_dustmap.py`; with no config file the page is a solo editor. Anonymous sign-in, Realtime Database, rules in `firebase/database.rules.json`.
   - A room is `rooms/<id>`, named in the link (`#room=<id>`). A bare visit (no `#room=`) opens this browser's **own** room (`localStorage['dustmap-own-room']`, set whenever this browser is a room's owner), falling back to the last room (`dustmap-room`) only if this browser owns it or nobody does, otherwise a fresh room. So opening Dustmap never drops you into someone else's room as a player; other rooms open only from their link. **Owner reclaims DM on open:** when the owner's page loads and the session's DM isn't connected (a closed test player, a friend from last week), the owner takes the DM role back automatically. The room is the group. Whoever first starts a session owns the room (`owner`), and the owner picks the DM (`dm`). Only those two can write the shared documents under `d/`. Everyone signed in reads. Each person writes only their own `people/<uid>/name` and `presence/<uid>_<tab>` (removed on disconnect).
   - **Group maps:** `mapIndex/<uid>_<local id>` (name, size, author, time) and `mapData/<same id>` (the map JSON). Anyone in the room can *Share with group* from the Map dialog; after that their edits sync up automatically (1.5 s debounce). Others *Open* a copy into their own library (re-opening refreshes that copy). Each person writes only their own entries; the room owner can also remove any.
   - Documents are stored flat as `d/<collection path with ~>/<doc id> = {j: JSON text}` (RTDB nests children, so `live/map` would otherwise wipe `live/map/parts`).
   - Ownership is tied to the browser's anonymous Firebase sign-in: clearing site data or another device means that browser is no longer the owner.
2. **claude.ai built-in** (when the page runs as an artifact): `db` + `room` + `user` capabilities. Publish with `capabilities: {db:{rules:[{path:"",read:"view",write:"admin"}]}, room:{}, user:{scopes:["profile"]}, downloads:true}`. Players must be signed in and invited by email.

Shared documents: `live/session {live, dm, assign:{<sheet>:<user id>}}`, `live/map {rev,n,name}` + `live/map/parts/p<i> {rev,s}` (the map minus tokens as ~150 KB text chunks), `live/tokens {rev, list:[{id,s,x,y,o,r}]}`.

- **DM's page is the source of truth**: edits, token moves and library map switches are pushed (debounced 250 ms).
- **Players** never write shared documents. Cursor, pointer trail, measure, live token drag, pings and finished moves go in presence. The DM's page checks a finished move (`mv`) against the assignment and commits it; a move made from a stale view (base rev older than the token's last change `r`) is dropped.
- **Player view:** Select (own cowboy only), Measure, Pointer, Pan; editing controls hidden (`.dmonly`, `body.mp-player`). The shared map never overwrites the player's own saved maps; ending the session brings theirs back.
- **Solo testing:** as DM (Firebase), *Play together → Open a test player* opens `?as=player#room=…` in a new tab: a throwaway player with its own anonymous identity and an in-memory copy of localStorage, so the DM's saved maps are never touched. The test player must use its own named Firebase app (`'testplayer'`, persistence NONE): on the default app it picked up the DM's saved sign-in and `setPersistence(NONE)` moved it out of storage, which locked the DM out of their room and made the "player" the same person as the DM. The test player tab is marked by its window name (`dustmap-test-player`), not its address: an old `?as=player` link still works but the marker is stripped from the URL at once, so reopening Dustmap from history or the address bar is always the real person (the DM). Its dialog has *Close test player*. Test players flag themselves in presence (`tp`), and nobody is offered *Make DM* for them (a test player tab closing would leave the table without a DM).
- **Players pick their own token:** a player with no token gets the picker (every cowboy and NPC, grouped as in the Tokens menu; taken ones are disabled). A click selects (`mp.pickSel`); *Play as <token>* (or a double click) sends it, and closing the dialog sends an unsent selection. The pick goes out on presence (`pick`); the DM's page (`mpPicks`) assigns it if free, drops that player's other assignment, and places the token near the others (`mpFreeSpot`) if the map has none. One token per player. There is no DM step, but the DM's page must be open: while it isn't, the picker says the pick goes through once it is. The dialog closes once the pick lands.
- **Player dialog:** players see the picker, their character, who's playing what, and Copy room link. *End session* is DM-only (the room's owner takes DM back first with *Take over as DM*, shown at the top, as a primary button when the DM isn't connected). *Start my own room* only shows when no session is on.
- **Start my own room:** anyone who isn't the room's owner sees it in Play together when no session is on (Firebase only). It makes a fresh room id, so a person who lost their sign-in (cleared site data, new browser) can run sessions again. Old group maps stay reachable through the old room link.
- Tests: `app/tests/firebase_auth_test.js` runs against the real auth emulator (`firebase emulators:start --only auth`, port 9099) plus the database emulator, and covers the test player identity, picking, own rooms and the DM reopening their tab.
- **Roles:** assignments are per character (sheet index), so they survive map switches. The owner can make someone else DM (their page saves the shared map as "Session map" in their library and carries on) and take it back.
- Tests: `multiplayer_test.js` (claude.ai transport, mocked) and `firebase_multiplayer_test.js` (Firebase transport + rules, against the local database emulator: `java -jar firebase-database-emulator-*.jar --port 9000`, with `FIREBASE_SDK` pointing at an installed `firebase@10.12.2`).

## Characters from D&D Beyond

- Any token can carry a character card: name, race, classes, level, HP (current / max / temp), AC, speed, the six scores and the portrait. The DM opens it from the selection bar, the right-click menu or a double-click; a player opens their own from their tray or Play together.
- **Import:** paste the character link (`dndbeyond.com/characters/<id>`, share links and bare ids work too). The page tries `character-service.dndbeyond.com/character/v5/character/<id>` first, but D&D Beyond only allows its own site to read it from a browser (CORS), so normally the dialog falls back to: open that URL in a new tab, copy everything, paste it back. Only **Public** characters work (private / campaign-only return `success:false`, shown as a "make it public" message). The endpoint is unofficial and may change.
- `DDB_PROXY` (top of the characters section in `dustmap_src.html`) is empty; set it to a server URL containing `{id}` that returns the same JSON (e.g. a route on the Vercel site) and the import becomes one step. D&D Beyond's terms discourage automated access, so this was left off.
- `ddbParse` derives the numbers: ability scores from base + racial/feat/item bonuses (items only when equipped and attuned) or overrides; HP = base + (CON mod + per-level bonuses) × level + bonus, or the override, minus damage taken; AC from worn armor (light + DEX, medium + DEX ≤ 2, heavy), unarmored defense, shield, bonuses and the sheet's AC override (`characterValues` typeId 1, magic 2, misc 3). Only the summary (`charClean`, < 1 KB) is kept or shared, never the full JSON.
- Storage: the DM's (and solo) cards by sheet index in `localStorage['dustmap-chars']`; a player's own in `dustmap-mychar`. Live: the player's card goes out on presence (`ch:{c,n}`), the DM's page takes it for the token they play, and publishes `live/chars {c:{<sheet>:summary}}` (only tokens someone plays or can see). HP/AC edits by the DM flow back to the player's copy. Everyone sees an HP bar under character tokens, and name / HP / AC on hover.
- Test: `app/tests/ddb_import_test.js` with `app/tests/fixtures/ddb_character.json`.

## Known limitations

- Full redraws are slow on 60×40 maps; maps above 60×40 are disabled.
- Stamps are per browser, not per map.
- Picked-up auto items are tied to the current layout; Reroll or reshaping a town can bring generated items back elsewhere.
- Live sessions: the DM's page must be open for players' token moves to be committed.
- Snapshot can't download directly (artifact sandbox); the user right-clicks the image.
